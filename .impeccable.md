## Design Context

### Users

Potential clients, collaborators, and viewers visit to understand Mei's visual practice quickly and browse selected work without interface distraction.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, editorial, and assured. The experience should feel like entering a small contemporary photography gallery: neutral architecture, precise pacing, and artwork-led color.

### Aesthetic Direction

Closely study the restrained portfolio system of Ittetsu Matsuoka: a white canvas, near-black typography, generous space, four-column desktop exhibition, single-column mobile reading flow, and subtle state transitions. Avoid generic cards, shadows, decorative gradients, dense UI, and motion that competes with the work.

### Design Principles

- Artwork is always the strongest visual element.
- The first visit opens on a full-bleed portrait, an original white botanical line drawing, and a fleeting name before yielding to the gallery.
- Space establishes rhythm and hierarchy; decoration does not.
- Loading follows reading order and reveals each completed image from top to bottom.
- Selecting a work is a spatial transition: competing works leave before navigation.
- Mobile is an intentional viewing mode, not a compressed desktop grid.
